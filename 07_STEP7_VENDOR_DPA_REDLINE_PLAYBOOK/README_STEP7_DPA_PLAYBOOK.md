# 📑 FOLDER 07: Step 7 - Vendor DPA Redline Playbook

### What is in this folder?
- `VENDOR_DPA_REDLINE_PLAYBOOK.md`: The TPRM negotiation playbook with 7 clause-by-clause contract redlines.

### Plain English Explanation:
- **What is this?** When our audit caught shadow vendors (Microsoft Clarity, Tapad, Reddit, LinkedIn), what does a privacy lawyer or privacy engineer do? You do not just complain—you fix the contract!
- **How it works:** Under GDPR Article 28, vendors must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). This playbook shows:
  - The vendor's standard sneaky boilerplate text.
  - Our aggressive privacy redline (strikethroughs and bold additions).
  - For example: **Clause 1** forbids vendors from training AI models on our user sessions; **Clause 2** forces a strict 48-hour breach notification SLA.
