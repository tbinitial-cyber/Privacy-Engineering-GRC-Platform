## PREAMBLE & RECITALS

This Data Processing Addendum, together with its Schedules and Annexes ("**DPA**"), is entered into by and between:

1. **THE CUSTOMER** (as defined in the Master Services Agreement, acting on its own behalf and, to the extent permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law, as agent for each Customer Affiliate), hereinafter referred to as the "**Customer**" or "**Data Controller**"; and
2. **THE CLOUD / SAAS VENDOR** (as defined in the Master Services Agreement, on behalf of itself and its Affiliates), hereinafter referred to as the "**Vendor**" or "**Data Processor**".

Customer and Vendor are each referred to individually as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

### RECITALS:
* **WHEREAS**, Customer and Vendor have entered into a Master Services Agreement, Terms of Service, or Order Form (the "**Agreement**") pursuant to which Vendor provides cloud-based software, infrastructure, and hosted platform services (the "**Services**") to Customer;
* **WHEREAS**, the provision of the Services entails the Processing of Customer Personal Data by Vendor on behalf of Customer;
* **WHEREAS**, the Parties desire to supplement the Agreement by entering into this DPA to ensure compliance with European Union Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("**GDPR**"), the United Kingdom Data Protection Act 2018 ("**UK GDPR**"), the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("**DPDPA 2023**"), and the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 ("**CCPA/CPRA**");

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained and other good and valuable consideration, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

In this DPA, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

* **1.1 "Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with a Party, where "Control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests.
* **1.2 "Applicable Data Protection Law"** means all worldwide privacy, data protection, and information security laws and regulations applicable to the Processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including, but not limited to: (i) the EU GDPR, (ii) the UK GDPR, (iii) the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection ("FADP"), (iv) the Indian DPDPA 2023, and (v) United States federal and state privacy statutes, including the CCPA/CPRA.
* **1.3 "Customer Personal Data"** means any Personal Data processed by Vendor or its Subprocessors on behalf of Customer in connection with the provision and receipt of the Services under the Agreement.
* **1.4 "Data Controller" or "Data Fiduciary"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of the Processing of Personal Data (or under DPDPA 2023, determines the purpose and means of Processing digital personal data).
* **1.5 "Data Processor"** means an entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Data Controller or Data Fiduciary.
* **1.6 "Data Subject" or "Data Principal"** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom Customer Personal Data relates.
* **1.7 "Personal Data Breach" or "Security Incident"** means a confirmed or reasonably suspected breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise Processed by Vendor or its Subprocessors.
* **1.8 "Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs"** means the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission in Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 for the transfer of personal data to third countries, specifically incorporating **Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor)**.
* **1.9 "Subprocessor"** means any third party (including any Vendor Affiliate) appointed by or on behalf of Vendor to Process Customer Personal Data in connection with the Agreement.
* **1.10 "Technical and Organisational Measures" or "TOMs"** means those administrative, physical, technical, and operational safeguards designed to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure or access, as detailed in **Schedule B**.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES OF THE PARTIES

* **2.1 Role Designation:** The Parties acknowledge and agree that for the purposes of GDPR Article 28, DPDPA 2023 Section 8, and CCPA/CPRA:
  * (a) Customer is the **Data Controller / Data Fiduciary** with respect to Customer Personal Data; and
  * (b) Vendor is the **Data Processor** acting solely on behalf of, and pursuant to the instructions of, Customer.
* **2.2 Customer Warranties:** Customer warrants that: (i) it has obtained all necessary statutory consents, notices, and lawful authorities required under Applicable Data Protection Law to transfer Customer Personal Data to Vendor for the purposes of the Services; and (ii) its documented instructions to Vendor comply with all Applicable Data Protection Laws.
* **2.3 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) Certifications:** To the extent CCPA applies to the Processing of Customer Personal Data:
  * (a) Vendor acts as a **Service Provider** (as defined under Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140(ag));
  * (b) Vendor shall not **Sell** or **Share** Customer Personal Data (as defined in § 1798.140(ad) and (ah));
  * (c) Vendor shall not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than the specific business purposes specified in the Agreement;
  * (d) Vendor shall not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Vendor and Customer; and
  * (e) Vendor shall not combine Customer Personal Data with personal information received from, or on behalf of, any other person or third party, except as expressly permitted under CPRA regulations.

## 3. PURPOSE LIMITATION & PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS

* **3.1 Documented Instructions:** Vendor shall Process Customer Personal Data solely on documented instructions from Customer, including with respect to international transfers, as set forth in the Agreement, this DPA, and any applicable Statement of Work or Order Form, unless required to do so by applicable European Union, Member State, or Indian law to which Vendor is subject. In such a case, Vendor shall inform Customer of that legal requirement before Processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.
* **3.2 Notification of Unlawful Instructions:** Vendor shall immediately inform Customer in writing if, in its reasonable opinion, any instruction provided by Customer infringes the GDPR, DPDPA 2023, or other Applicable Data Protection Law.
* **3.3 Absolute Prohibition on AI Model Training & Telemetry Commercialization:** 
  * (a) Vendor covenants and warrants that it shall **NOT** use, extract, analyze, or process Customer Personal Data (including user sessions, whiteboard canvas content, customer inputs, chat transcripts, or uploaded files) to train, re-train, fine-tune, optimize, or evaluate any artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, or large language foundation models, whether owned by Vendor or any third party.
  * (b) Customer Personal Data shall not be aggregated, combined, or pooled with third-party datasets for marketing, profiling, behavioral targeting, or product development purposes.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY OF PERSONNEL

Vendor shall ensure that any employee, contractor, agent, or representative who has access to Customer Personal Data:
* (a) Is informed of the strictly confidential nature of Customer Personal Data;
* (b) Has received adequate training on statutory data protection obligations and information security standards; and
* (c) Is bound by enforceable statutory or contractual obligations of confidentiality that survive the termination of their employment or engagement.

## 5. SECURITY OF PROCESSING (TOMs)

* **5.1 Implementation of Safeguards:** Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of Processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, Vendor shall implement and maintain appropriate **Technical and Organisational Measures** pursuant to GDPR Article 32 and DPDPA 2023 Section 8(5), as specified in **Schedule B** attached hereto.
* **5.2 Minimum Standards:** Such measures shall at a minimum include:
  * (a) The pseudonymisation and encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (using modern Transport Layer Security TLS 1.3 or minimum TLS 1.2) and at rest (using minimum Advanced Encryption Standard AES-256);
  * (b) The ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems and services;
  * (c) The ability to restore the availability and access to Customer Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident; and
  * (d) A process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organisational measures for ensuring the security of the processing.

## 6. SUBPROCESSORS

* **6.1 General Authorization:** Customer hereby grants general written authorization to Vendor to appoint Subprocessors to perform specific processing activities on behalf of Customer, subject to the conditions set forth in this Section 6.
* **6.2 Approved Subprocessors:** Customer explicitly approves the appointment of the Subprocessors listed in **Schedule D** as of the Effective Date of this DPA.
* **6.3 Prior Notice of Changes:** Vendor shall give Customer **at least thirty (30) calendar days’ prior written notice** (via email notification to Customer's designated administrative or privacy contact, or via an automated subscription portal) of any intended appointment of a new Subprocessor or replacement of an existing Subprocessor.
* **6.4 Right to Object:** Customer shall have the right to object in writing to the appointment of any new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within fourteen (14) days of receiving such notice. If Customer objects:
  * (a) Vendor shall use reasonable endeavors to provide an alternative configuration or solution that avoids processing by the contested Subprocessor;
  * (b) If Vendor is unable to provide such an alternative within thirty (30) days of Customer's objection notice, Customer may terminate the Agreement and this DPA with respect to the affected Services immediately upon written notice, without penalty or termination fee, and receive a pro-rata refund of any prepaid, unearned fees.
* **6.5 Subprocessor Contractual Flow-Down:** Vendor shall enter into a formal, binding written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those imposed on Vendor under this DPA (including the requirements of GDPR Article 28(3) and SCC Module 2).
* **6.6 Primary Liability:** Vendor shall remain fully liable to Customer for the performance of each Subprocessor’s obligations and for any acts, omissions, or defaults of each Subprocessor that result in a breach of this DPA or Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS ASSISTANCE (DSAR)

* **7.1 Non-Direct Interaction:** Vendor shall not directly respond to any request, inquiry, or complaint received from a Data Subject regarding Customer Personal Data, and shall instead forward such communication to Customer's designated privacy contact within **forty-eight (48) hours** of receipt.
* **7.2 Technical Assistance & SLA:** Taking into account the nature of Processing, Vendor shall assist Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling Customer's statutory obligation to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under GDPR Articles 15–22, DPDPA 2023 Sections 11–14, and CCPA/CPRA (including access, correction, erasure, portability, and restriction requests).
* **7.3 Expedited Turnaround:** Where self-service administrative tools are insufficient to extract or erase Customer Personal Data, Vendor shall, upon written request from Customer, locate, export, or permanently delete the identified records across all production databases and microservices within **five (5) business days**.

---

## 8. PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION & INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

* **8.1 Strict Notification Window:** In the event of a confirmed or reasonably suspected Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, Vendor shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event **no later than forty-eight (48) hours** after Vendor becomes aware of such breach.
* **8.2 Minimum Report Contents:** Such notification shall at a minimum contain:
  * (a) A detailed description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
  * (b) The name and contact details of Vendor’s Data Protection Officer or other contact point where more information can be obtained;
  * (c) The likely consequences and potential risks of the breach to affected Data Subjects; and
  * (d) The measures taken or planned to be taken by Vendor to address and mitigate the breach.
* **8.3 Mitigation & Cooperation:** Vendor shall immediately take all necessary technical steps to contain, control, and remediate the Personal Data Breach at its own expense. Vendor shall fully cooperate with Customer and assist Customer in meeting Customer’s statutory reporting obligations to competent Supervisory Authorities (such as the 72-hour GDPR Article 33 notice) and to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).

## 9. DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS (DPIA) & PRIOR CONSULTATION

Vendor shall, upon Customer's written request and taking into account the nature of Processing and information available to Vendor, provide reasonable assistance to Customer in carrying out:
* (a) Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) pursuant to GDPR Article 35 and DPDPA 2023 Section 10(2)(c); and
* (b) Prior consultations with competent Supervisory Authorities pursuant to GDPR Article 36.

---

## 10. AUDIT AND VERIFICATION RIGHTS

* **10.1 Demonstration of Compliance:** Vendor shall make available to Customer on an annual basis all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Law.
* **10.2 Third-Party Audit Reports:** Vendor satisfies its obligation under Section 10.1 by providing Customer with an executive summary of its current **SOC 2 Type II** audit report (certifying Security, Confidentiality, and Availability), **ISO/IEC 27001** certification, and annual third-party penetration test summaries.
* **10.3 Customer Audits:** Customer (or an independent, certified third-party auditor bound by strict confidentiality) may, upon at least fourteen (14) business days’ prior written notice, conduct a remote or on-site audit of Vendor’s processing facilities and systems to verify compliance with this DPA. Such audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably disrupt Vendor’s operations.
* **10.4 Immediate Audit Trigger:** Customer shall have the right to conduct an immediate audit without regard to the 14-day notice period in the event of: (i) a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data; or (ii) a formal instruction or order from a competent Supervisory Authority.

## 11. INTERNATIONAL CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS

* **11.1 Prohibition on Unlawful Transfers:** Vendor shall not transfer Customer Personal Data across international borders unless such transfer strictly complies with Chapter V of the GDPR, UK Data Protection Law, and Indian DPDPA Section 16.
* **11.2 Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** Where Customer Personal Data originating in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland is transferred to Vendor or its Subprocessors in a third country not recognized as providing an adequate level of data protection:
  * (a) The Parties hereby agree that the **European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor)** are incorporated herein by reference and form an integral part of this DPA;
  * (b) Customer acts as the "data exporter" and Vendor acts as the "data importer";
  * (c) The details of the processing operations set forth in **Schedule A** and the security measures in **Schedule B** shall serve as Annex I and Annex II to the SCCs, respectively.
* **11.3 UK and Swiss Transfers:** For transfers originating in the UK, the UK Information Commissioner's International Data Transfer Addendum ("**UK IDTA**") is incorporated herein. For Swiss transfers, references to GDPR shall be construed as references to the Swiss FADP.

## 12. GOVERNMENT ACCESS REQUESTS & LAW ENFORCEMENT

* **12.1 Notice Requirement:** If Vendor receives a legally binding subpoena, warrant, national security letter, or court order from a law enforcement or government authority demanding disclosure of Customer Personal Data (including under the US CLOUD Act or FISA Section 702), Vendor shall:
  * (a) Promptly notify Customer in writing prior to disclosing any data, unless legally prohibited from doing so by a valid statutory gag order;
  * (b) Direct the requesting authority to request the data directly from Customer; and
  * (c) Inform the authority that Vendor processes Customer Personal Data solely on behalf of Customer.
* **12.2 Duty to Challenge Overbroad Orders:** Vendor warrants that it shall review each government request on a case-by-case basis and will use all available legal remedies to challenge, contest, and appeal any order or request that is overbroad, arbitrary, or contrary to fundamental human rights under international law.
* **12.3 Data Minimization:** Where disclosure is legally compelled and cannot be avoided, Vendor shall disclose only the absolute minimum amount of Customer Personal Data necessary to satisfy the legal order.

---

## 13. DATA RETURN, DELETION & CERTIFIED DESTRUCTION

* **13.1 Deletion SLA:** Following termination or expiration of the Agreement, or upon Customer’s written request at any time during the term, Vendor shall, at Customer’s choice, either:
  * (a) Securely return all Customer Personal Data to Customer in a standardized, machine-readable format (e.g. JSON or CSV); or
  * (b) Permanently and irreversibly delete all Customer Personal Data, including all existing copies, fragments, and active backups, within **thirty (30) calendar days**.
* **13.2 Rotating Backup Purge:** Vendor shall ensure that any residual customer data residing in rotating disaster recovery backups is completely overwritten and purged within a maximum of **sixty (60) calendar days** post-termination.
* **13.3 Certificate of Destruction:** Vendor’s Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or designated corporate officer shall furnish Customer with a signed, written **Certificate of Destruction** within forty-five (45) days of contract termination, certifying that all physical and logical media containing Customer Personal Data have been irreversibly sanitized in accordance with **NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1** Guidelines for Media Sanitization.

---

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & MISCELLANEOUS

* **14.1 Order of Precedence:** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the terms of this DPA and the Master Services Agreement, the terms of this DPA shall prevail with respect to data protection matters. To the extent the Standard Contractual Clauses apply, the SCCs shall prevail over all other agreements.
* **14.2 Enterprise Data Protection Super-Cap:** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Master Services Agreement, the total aggregate liability of either Party arising out of or related to a breach of this DPA, Applicable Data Protection Law, or a Personal Data Breach shall be subject to a separate, dedicated **Super-Cap equal to three times (3x) the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident**.
* **14.3 Severability:** If any provision of this DPA is held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the validity of the remaining provisions shall not be affected.
* **14.4 Governing Law and Jurisdiction:** This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the governing law and jurisdiction specified in the Master Services Agreement, provided that where the SCCs apply, the governing law specified in Clause 17 of the SCCs shall control.

## SCHEDULE A: DETAILS OF PROCESSING OPERATIONS

### 1. Categories of Data Subjects:
* Employees, contractors, and authorized workforce users of Customer;
* Customers, prospective clients, and business partners of Customer;
* Website visitors and digital platform end-users.

### 2. Categories of Customer Personal Data:
* **Identity & Contact Data:** Name, business email address, phone number, corporate job title, physical address;
* **Technical & Network Telemetry:** IP address, device fingerprints, unique cookie identifiers (`CLID`, `_ga`, session tokens), browser User-Agent strings, access timestamps, and telemetry logs;
* **Workspace & Customer Content:** Digital collaboration canvas content, uploaded documents, ticket metadata, user-submitted form responses, and chat transcripts.

### 3. Special Categories of Personal Data (GDPR Article 9 / DPDPA Sensitive Data):
* None intended or anticipated. Customer is instructed not to input health, biometric, genetic, or political data into the Services.

### 4. Nature and Purpose of the Processing:
* The provision, maintenance, hosting, technical support, and continuous operation of the cloud-based collaborative software platform as requested by Customer.

### 5. Duration of Processing and Retention Period:
* For the duration of the Agreement plus thirty (30) days for operational decommissioning, unless required to be retained longer under mandatory statutory retention laws (e.g. tax or accounting rules).
