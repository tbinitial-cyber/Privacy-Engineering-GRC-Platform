# 🏛️ FOLDER 05: Step 5 - Official Records of Processing Activities (RoPA)

### What is in this folder?
- `ROPA_ARTICLE_30_REGISTER.xlsx`: The 4-sheet corporate Excel workbook (Governance Summary, RoPA Register, Subprocessors & Transfers, Evidence Traceability).
- `ROPA_ARTICLE_30_REGISTER.md`: The complete publication-grade Markdown version.

### Plain English Explanation:
- **What is a RoPA?** Under GDPR Article 30 and Indian DPDPA Section 8, every company MUST maintain an official logbook of all personal data activities. If a data protection authority (like the European DPAs or Indian Data Protection Board) knocks on the door, this is the first document they demand.
- **What did we do?** We took the 5 candidate activities from Step 3, verified the evidence from Step 2 and Step 4, and assigned the formal lawful bases:
  - *Consent (GDPR Art 6(1)(a) / DPDPA Sec 6)* for Advertising and Analytics.
  - *Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art 6(1)(f) / DPDPA Sec 7)* for strictly necessary security (Cloudflare/OneTrust).
  - *Contract / Pre-contractual (GDPR Art 6(1)(b))* for Intercom customer support chat.
